This started at 3 AM with a voice memo and six tabs open trying to figure out which free plugin wouldn't crash this time.

I was working on songs in a subscription DAW when my payment lapsed. Just a gap. But my sessions were tied to that program — months of my own work, locked behind a billing cycle I couldn't afford to renew. Couldn't afford the five other subscriptions it takes to do what every musician does — hear something in your head and try to get it out.

So I built the thing I needed. A program that feels like making music feels. Not clinical. Not complicated. Not designed by people who've never stayed up all night chasing a melody that won't let you sleep.

It watches what you're doing and shows you the right tools. It separates stems with AI. It writes your lyrics down while you sing. It does everything the expensive programs do, and some things they can't.

And it costs less than two months of a subscription DAW. Once.

  • Record Multi-track recording with click track, count-in, and input monitoring. Low-latency ASIO. Plug in and play.
  • AI Stem Split Drop any song. Get back vocals, drums, bass, guitar, piano, and more — each on its own track. Learn from the greats by hearing what they did.
  • Lyrics Hit transcribe and it writes the words as the song plays. Timestamps and all. Export as LRC, SRT, or plain text.
  • Mix & Master EQ, compression, reverb, delay, limiting. LUFS metering for streaming platforms. A/B against reference tracks.
  • Edit by Feel Split, trim, stretch, pitch shift, crossfade. Every tool has plain-English tooltips. Hover anything to learn what it does.
  • Adaptive UI The program reads the room. Recording? Recording tools. Mixing? EQ and compressor come forward. No modes. No hunting. It just knows.
  • Key & BPM Load a track and instantly know the key, tempo, loudness, and duration. Match tempos between tracks. Build from what's already there.
  • Export WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG, AIFF. Any sample rate, any bit depth, with dithering. Stem export. One click.
