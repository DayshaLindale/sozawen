Notes (whole through sixteenth), rests, dynamics (ppp-fff), articulations (staccato, accent, tenuto, fermata, marcato, trill), ties, slurs, crescendo/decrescendo hairpins, grace notes, dotted notes, triplets, beaming, bar lines, repeat signs, volta brackets, tempo markings, pedal markings, segno/coda/D.C./D.S., ottava (8va/8vb).   • Bleed RemovalRecording guitar DI and vocals at the same time? The mic picks up guitar bleed. Sozawen uses the clean DI signal to mathematically subtract the bleed. Same tech as noise-cancelling headphones.

Ask for a rock drumbeat → it writes one into the drum machine. Ask for a Steinway playing C-F-G-Am → the notes render through the piano into a new track. Ask it to analyze a track, save a mixer snapshot, open the score editor, quantize a MIDI take, or shift a take's pitch — it runs the tool for you. Built on a locally-bundled model, no cloud, no account.

  • Edit by FeelSplit, trim, stretch, pitch shift, crossfade. Click-drag to select ranges. Every tool has plain-English tooltips that teach the hardware equivalent.
  • Adaptive UIThe program reads the room. Recording? Recording tools appear. Mixing? EQ and compressor come forward. No modes. No menus. It just knows.
  • Key & BPMLoad a track and instantly know the key, tempo, loudness, and duration. Match tempos between tracks. Build from what's already there.
  • Synth & PadSubtractive synth with 4 waveforms, filter, ADSR. MPC-style pad grid mapped to your scale — tap to play, hold multiple for chords. Plus 16 keyboard instruments (Rhodes Mark I/II, Wurlitzer, Clavinet, Hammond B3 with 9 drawbars, key click, percussion, and Leslie speaker simulation).
  • Drum Machine18 modal-synthesis drum sounds built from real membrane physics — kick (8ms pitch sweep, membrane modes, shell resonance), snare (buzz wires), hi-hats (open/closed/pedal), toms, claps, rides, crashes, chinas, splashes, double kick. Step sequencer with 26 genre presets. Stateful cymbals remember they are still vibrating between strikes. No samples.
  • SidechainDuck one track based on another's level. Classic kick-ducking-bass. Select trigger and target, set threshold and ratio.
  • Snap & MarkersSnap-to-grid (bar to 1/16). Named markers on the timeline. Jump between sections with [ ] keys.
  • AutomationDraw volume and pan curves on the waveform. Click to add points. Visual SVG overlay. Per-track, per-parameter.
  • Piano RollVisual MIDI editor — click a grid to place notes, set octave and resolution, transpose, render through the synth. Draw melodies and chords by hand.
  • Chord DetectionAnalyzes your audio and identifies what chords are playing at each moment. Click any chord to seek to that point. 9 chord types across all 12 keys.
  • Audio to MIDIConverts a monophonic recording (vocal, bass, lead) into MIDI notes. Render the detected melody through the synth or transpose it to a new key.
  • Noise ReductionSpectral denoising — removes steady-state noise like hiss, room tone, and fan noise. Same principle as iZotope RX. Learns from the quiet parts of your recording.
  • TunerChromatic tuner with note name, frequency, and cents display. Tune your instrument right inside the DAW. Shows how many cents sharp or flat you are.
  • MetronomeClick track during playback and recording. Downbeat accent. Tap tempo to set BPM by feel. Count-in bars before recording starts.
  • Sample BrowserBrowse folders on your computer for audio files. See names and sizes. Click to add to a track. Find your sounds without leaving the DAW.
  • VST3 + CLAPScan the system for installed plugins, insert on any track, process live during playback. Every parameter exposes as a slider. Bypass per-plugin without unloading. Save current state as a preset and reload on any track. Pedalboard hosts VST3 and CLAP alike.
  • Immersive 7.1.4 / 5.1.2Full multichannel immersive rendering — 12-channel 7.1.4 bed or 8-channel 5.1.2, plus binaural downmix for headphones. Drag each track's 3D position in the spatial panner. Output includes ADM metadata sidecar compatible with the Dolby Atmos Renderer and Logic so their object-based engines can read your placements.
  • Cymbal State MemoryHi-hats, crashes, rides, chinas, and splashes remember they are still vibrating. A second strike on a ringing cymbal sums nonlinearly with phase coherence — closely-spaced cymbal hits sound like a real drummer, not re-triggered samples. Per-mode amplitude + phase tracked across every hit.

  • Music Theory20 scales, 18 chord types, circle of fifths, diatonic chord builder, 10 common progressions (pop, blues, jazz, rock). Key compatibility analysis. All accessible via API and the Bandmate.
